# RELAY interaction direction

Distilled from the supplied VOVERE development conversation (8 October 2026).
The source is a design reference, not a list of commands to replay in VOVERE.

## What carries across

- Preserve the coherent monochrome foundation; repair weak interactions instead of repeatedly redesigning the whole product.
- Animate the thing being used. Material and readable content arrive together. No blank expanding plates, arbitrary travelling icons or repeated decorative flourishes.
- Give each action an understandable beginning, pending state and actual result. Success follows the server response. Errors keep input and provide a clear retry. No fake loading delay.
- Compose mobile navigation independently: reachable controls, rounded surfaces, safe areas, clear current location and a predictable way back.
- Give consequential actions a review step. Explain what changes, what is retained and how recovery works.
- Keep motion short and interruptible. Honor reduced motion, keyboard focus, native scrolling and browser back behavior.
- Judge typography in the rendered UI: full descenders, readable labels, no accidental narrow columns or clipped names.
- Use technology to improve the result, not to justify a Three.js/GSAP dependency. A dashboard needs fast interaction, not a cinematic intro before work.
- Prove quality with actual UI and data-flow checks. A successful build does not prove every device or thousands of concurrent users.

## RELAY-specific translation

Neutral surfaces remain the foundation. Mint/green signals a completed operation; warm red signals a recoverable problem or important consequence. These colors are not decorative branding throughout the dashboard.

The overview is the live preview for customization. Supplier removal is recoverable through Trash; documents are retained, active access is revoked, and restoration never silently reopens old links. User input and company isolation matter as much as motion.

VOVERE's cinematic scenes, creative imagery and historic color treatments are not requirements for RELAY. The retired Brand OS and Emil references were not used for this pass.
